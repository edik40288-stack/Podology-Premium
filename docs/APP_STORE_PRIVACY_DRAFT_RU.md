# App Store privacy draft

Черновик для App Privacy в App Store Connect. Финально сверить с реальной production privacy policy.

## Data linked to user

- Contact Info: name, phone number.
- User Content: messages, user-selected photos/videos.
- Identifiers: app session/auth token stored securely on device.

## Data use

- App functionality.
- Customer support and center communication.
- Booking management.

## Tracking

No tracking across apps and websites. No advertising ID usage. No third-party advertising SDK.

## Sensitive health data

The app should not claim to collect Apple HealthKit data or clinical health records. User-selected photos/videos are used for communication with the center and are not collected from HealthKit.
